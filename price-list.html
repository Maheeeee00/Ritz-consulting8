From 1 July 2026, Victoria replaced Domestic Building Insurance (DBI) eligibility with Home Warranty and the Minimum Financial Requirements (MFR). These services cover new MFR applications, Maximum Construction Capacity (MCC) increases, reviews, and any remaining DBI eligibility work for contracts signed before that date. Most builders fit Tier 2.

Terms

  • Builder tiers: 50% on engagement, balance on submission to the distributor. Fees cover our work and are payable whatever BPC decides.
  • Advisory: initial fee upfront; monthly fee in advance by direct debit.
  • Compliance: invoiced on lodgement; direct debit available for BAS and monthly clients.
  • Hourly rates for work outside scope: Principal $350 · Senior accountant $250 · Accountant $180.
  • Out-of-scope advisory: $300–$400 per hour or $1,800–$2,500 per day (lender negotiations, refinancing, formal restructuring support).
  • Not included: BPC premiums, ASIC fees and third-party fees, which are passed on at cost.
  • Fees valid to 30 June 2027.
